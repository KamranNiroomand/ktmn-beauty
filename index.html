About

A small team, run carefully.

KTMN Beauty is an independent import and wholesale business. We connect verified skincare supply from Korea, Japan, Europe, Canada, and the US with retailers, pharmacies, and resellers serving the Gulf region.

We work directly with export-accredited partners and brand-authorized distributors, and we're upfront about it when we can't: if a brand doesn't have a clear wholesale channel for the UAE, we say so rather than pointing you toward unverified stock.
